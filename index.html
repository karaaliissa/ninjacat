What is Ninjacat, in one sentence?+

An independent, community-led Web3 project building a universe of games, stories, collectibles and products around a ninja-cat character.

Do I need a token or wallet to explore?+

No. You can discover the story, browse the artwork and visit public community channels without connecting a wallet. Some separate ecosystem services may have their own wallet or eligibility requirements.

Is this an official Microsoft project?+

No. The character’s story draws inspiration from Microsoft’s historical unofficial Ninja Cat mascot. This is an independent community project; use of historical references does not establish a partnership or endorsement.

What is available, and what is planned?+

The existing project has public community channels, an origin comic, an archive, an arcade and a token-analysis interface. NinjaSwap, broader entertainment, merchandise and further integrations are described as developing or planned. Availability and functionality should be checked on the linked project pages.

How do the MSFT rewards work?+

The project describes an MSFT-linked rewards initiative for eligible holders. Check the original dashboard for the exact asset, eligibility, supported regions and distribution terms. This preview does not verify reward delivery or promise returns.

What about the charity initiative?+

The project’s published initiative describes support for a Japanese cat sanctuary and children’s martial arts activities. The original website links to Japan Cat Network and Global Judo Kids Network donation pages.
